# All-Staff Notices — Known Issues Following Windows 11 Migration
**Date:** 2026-08-12

---

## Notice 1 — Sign-In and Account Lockouts

**To:** All Staff
**Subject:** Some users are having trouble signing in this morning — we are on it

We are aware that a number of colleagues are experiencing account lockouts this morning and our team is actively investigating. You do not need to raise a ticket — we already know about this and are working to resolve it.

**While we work on a fix, please try this first:**
- Wait 15 minutes and try signing in again — accounts can sometimes recover on their own.
- From a phone or another device, visit [aka.ms/sspr](https://aka.ms/sspr) to unlock your account yourself.

If you are still locked out after trying those steps, please contact the service desk and let them know your username and how many times you have been locked out today.

We will provide an update by **2pm today**. We know this is frustrating and we appreciate your patience — our team is focused on getting everyone back in as quickly as possible.

## Notice 2 — OneDrive Files Appearing Missing

**To:** All Staff
**Subject:** Some files may appear missing in OneDrive — please read before raising a ticket

We are aware that some colleagues are not seeing all of their files in OneDrive this morning following the Windows 11 migration. Our team is actively looking into this and you do not need to raise a ticket right now.

**While we investigate, please try this first:**
- Check the OneDrive icon in the bottom-right corner of your screen. A spinning circle means your files are still catching up — give it a few minutes.
- Visit [onedrive.com](https://onedrive.com) and check your Recycle Bin — files may be recoverable from there.

If your files are still missing after 30 minutes, or you have a deadline today that cannot wait, please contact the service desk straight away and tell them which files or folders are affected and when you last saw them.

We will share an update by **2pm today**. Please do not delete or move anything in OneDrive in the meantime, as this helps us recover files more easily.

## Notice 3 — Shared Drive Access (S: Drive)

**To:** All Staff
**Subject:** Some teams cannot access shared drives this morning — we are working on it

We are aware that some colleagues — particularly in Finance — are unable to access shared drives since the Windows 11 upgrade. Our team is already investigating and working to restore access. You do not need to raise a ticket at this stage.

**While we work on a fix, please try this first:**
- Sign out of your computer fully and sign back in. This can refresh your access and may restore the drive straight away.

If the drive is still missing after signing back in, please contact the service desk and tell them your name, the drive letter you cannot reach (e.g. S:), and any error message shown on screen.

We will provide an update by **2pm today**. We understand this is affecting important work and resolving it is our highest priority right now — thank you for bearing with us.
