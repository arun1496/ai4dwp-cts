# Self-Service Help — Top 3 Issues After Windows 11 Migration

---

## 1. My account is locked and I cannot log in

Your account may have locked automatically after too many sign-in attempts.

**Try this:**
1. Wait 15 minutes, then try signing in again — accounts sometimes unlock on their own after a short time.
2. If you have access to another device or your phone, visit the self-service password reset page at [aka.ms/sspr](https://aka.ms/sspr) to unlock your account yourself.

**Contact the service desk if:** you are still locked out, or this has happened more than once this week.
When you call or raise a ticket, tell them: your full name, your username, roughly when the lockout happened, and how many times it has occurred.

---

## 2. My files are missing or not showing up in OneDrive

After the move to Windows 11, some files may still be uploading or catching up in the background.

**Try this:**
1. Look for the OneDrive cloud icon in the bottom-right corner of your screen. If it shows a spinning circle or a pause symbol, your files are still syncing — wait a few minutes and check again.
2. Check your OneDrive Recycle Bin online at [onedrive.com](https://onedrive.com) — some files may be recoverable from there.

**Contact the service desk if:** files are still missing after 30 minutes, or you have a deadline today.
Tell them: your name, which files or folders are missing, and when you last saw them.

---

## 3. I cannot access the shared drive (e.g. the S: drive)

You may have lost access to a shared folder after the Windows 11 upgrade.

**Try this:**
1. Sign out of your computer completely and sign back in — this refreshes your permissions and sometimes restores access straight away.

**Contact the service desk if:** the drive is still missing after signing back in.
Tell them: your name, which drive letter or folder you cannot reach, and what error message (if any) appears on screen.
