# Leadership Update — Windows 11 Migration: Executive Summary
**Date:** 2026-08-12
**Audience:** Non-technical management
**Prepared by:** IT Service Delivery

---

The Windows 11 migration has largely gone well for our staff. The disruptions that affected users in the first week — including sign-in failures, missing files, loss of access to shared drives, and an unreliable VPN — have all been resolved. Login speeds are back to normal, files are syncing reliably, and the VPN has been stable throughout the second week. User sentiment has shifted noticeably: the majority of feedback in week two reflects a smooth and improved experience, with several staff commenting that the transition has been a positive change overall. The most serious early risks — potential data loss and complete work stoppages — were addressed quickly and without permanent impact.

Three items remain open and are being actively managed. The floor 3 printer has still not been restored since the upgrade; staff have a temporary workaround but frustration is growing and some are beginning to escalate — a firm resolution date is being set this week. A new issue has emerged in the Finance team where Excel is crashing or freezing on larger budget files, causing lost work during a critical period; this has been raised as a priority incident and is under investigation. Separately, Finance staff are experiencing noticeably slower computer start-up times following a configuration change applied on 4 August; the most likely cause has been identified and a small controlled test is running now to confirm the fix before it is applied more broadly. Updates on all three will follow as findings are confirmed.
