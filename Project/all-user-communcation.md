# Floor 6 User Update

Hello Floor 6 team,

We know some of you are having trouble signing in, seeing slow computers after sign-in, or missing desktop shortcuts. We understand how disruptive this is, and we are actively working on it now.

A separate concern was raised about Copilot showing a client matter. That concern is being reviewed separately as a security matter with the right teams.

## What you should do
- If sign-in is slow, wait for the desktop to fully load before opening apps.
- If shortcuts are missing, use Start menu search to open your apps.
- If you can, keep your device powered on and connected so we can complete checks.
- Report your issue with your name, device name, and what you saw (for example: slow sign-in, missing shortcuts, app not opening).

## What you should not do
- Do not repeatedly restart your device unless IT asks you to.
- Do not uninstall or reinstall apps yourself.
- Do not change security or account settings.
- Do not share client matter details in chat, email, or AI tools while the security review is in progress.

Thank you for your patience. We will continue to share updates as we confirm more information.
