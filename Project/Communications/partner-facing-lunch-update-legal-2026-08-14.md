## Current Situation
A group of Floor 6 Legal users reported three issues this morning: slow sign-in, occasional sign-in failure, and missing desktop shortcuts. The operational response is active and focused on stabilizing normal access and desktop use.

## Impact
- Some users are taking longer than normal to start work.
- A smaller number of users cannot complete sign-in on first attempt.
- Some users are missing shortcuts they use for daily tasks.
- Team productivity is reduced for affected users.

## What Is Being Done
- A joint service desk and endpoint engineering response is running now.
- A small pilot group of affected devices is being used first to confirm safe fixes.
- Evidence is being collected and compared with an unaffected device before wider changes are made.
- If pilot results are positive, changes will be expanded in controlled waves.
- User updates continue through the service desk communication channel.

## Data/Security Concern
A separate security and compliance review is open for the Copilot/client matter concern. That review is running in parallel and is managed as a distinct track from the access and desktop stability work.

## What Users Should Do
- Keep devices powered on and connected to company network or VPN.
- Save active work regularly.
- Follow service desk instructions if asked to restart.
- Report exact symptoms and device name to helpdesk.
- Do not install or remove apps, and do not run internet repair tools.

## What Remains Under Review
- Final confirmation of the primary technical trigger affecting this Floor 6 group.
- Validation of pilot results across all affected patterns.
- Security and compliance findings for the separate Copilot/client matter track.
