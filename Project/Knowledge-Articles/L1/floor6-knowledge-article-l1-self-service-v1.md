If your sign-in is slow, fails, or desktop shortcuts are missing, the support team is already running a controlled fix for Floor 6.

What to do:
- Keep your device on and connected to company network or VPN.
- Save your work often during this period.
- If asked by Service Desk, restart once and sign in again.
- Report your exact issue (slow login, failed login, or missing shortcut) and your device name.

What not to do:
- Do not install or remove apps yourself.
- Do not delete desktop folders or shortcuts.
- Do not run cleanup or repair tools from the internet.
- Do not repeatedly force reboot unless Service Desk asks.

Important: The Copilot/client matter concern is being handled in a separate security review. This article covers only login and desktop stability actions from the same Floor 6 runbook.
