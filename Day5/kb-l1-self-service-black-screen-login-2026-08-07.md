# L1 Help: Black Screen After You Sign In

v 1.0, 07/08/2026, status : Draft

If you sign in and only see a black screen, please do not worry. Your files and work are safe. This is a display problem only. It does not remove your saved documents.

## What you can do now

1. Wait up to 2 minutes.
The screen may appear after a short wait.

2. If the screen is still black, sign out.
Then close the work app fully.

3. Wait 1 minute, then open the work app and sign in again.

4. If it still does not work after 2 tries, stop trying and contact the service desk.

## Important

- Your data is safe even if the screen stays black.
- Trying to sign in many times can make it take longer to return to normal, so pause after 2 tries.

## Contact the service desk if...

Contact the service desk if the black screen is still there after 2 sign-in tries, or if you keep getting signed out.

## Details to include in your support request

- Your full name
- Your work login name or email
- Your location (office or home)
- The time the issue started
- Whether this happened once or more than once
- A short note of what you saw (for example: "black screen after sign-in")
- Whether signing out and trying again fixed it
- If possible, a screenshot of the black screen
