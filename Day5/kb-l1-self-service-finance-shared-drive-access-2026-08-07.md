# Finance Shared Folder Access Help (Do It Yourself)

**Version:** v 1.0  
**Date:** 07/08/2026  
**Status:** Draft

## What this is

Use these steps if your Finance shared folder is missing or will not open after you log in.

Your files are safe. This is usually a temporary delay, not files being lost.

## What you can do now

1. Save any open work in programs that are still working.
2. Sign out of Windows.
3. Wait 60 seconds.
4. Sign back in.
5. Open your folders window and check if your Finance shared folder is back.
6. If it is still missing, wait 10 minutes.
7. Sign out and sign back in one more time.
8. Check the Finance shared folder again.

## If it still does not work

Contact the IT Help Desk if the folder is still missing or will not open after trying to log in twice.

## Details to include in your help request

Please copy and share these details:

- Your full name
- Your work login name
- Your computer name (for example: DESKTOP-FB123)
- Your office location or floor
- The exact time the problem started
- What you saw (for example: “Finance folder missing” or “cannot open Finance folder”)
- The steps you already tried from this guide
- Whether this is happening to coworkers near you

## Reassurance

Your documents are not deleted. In most cases, access returns after you sign out, wait, and sign back in.
