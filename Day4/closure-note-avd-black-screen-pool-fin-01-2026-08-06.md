Resolved. Cause: the 02:00 POOL-FIN-01 image update introduced a defective Intel graphics driver component, igdumd64.dll, which caused DWM to crash during session initialisation and triggered the black screen. Action: affected POOL-FIN-01 hosts were placed in drain mode, new sessions were redirected to POOL-FIN-02, the graphics driver was rolled back to the last known-good version in the image, one host was rebuilt and validated, and the corrected image was rolled out to the remaining POOL-FIN-01 hosts. Preventive: require a canary host and mandatory post-login DWM/graphics stability validation before future image rollouts. User confirmed working.
